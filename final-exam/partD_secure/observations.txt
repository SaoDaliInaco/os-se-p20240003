Note: With stock=100 and 40 concurrent buyers (1 unit each), the correct final
stock should be 60. The unpatched buy_beacon_unsafe consistently produced 99 
across all 3 runs instead of 60. This is because the artificial sleep(0.1) widens 
the race window enough that all 40 processes read stock=100 BEFORE any of them 
writes back — each computes 100-1=99 independently, and since they all write 
to the same file, only the last write "wins," overwriting all the others. 
39 of the 40 purchases were silently lost. This demonstrates a severe 
TOCTOU (time-of-check-to-time-of-use) race condition.
